Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?

Calling only fullRefit keeps the tree correct, but boxes grow and
overlap as circles move, making pixel tracing slower over time.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?

Using reset every frame rebuilds the tree around the circles
current positions, keeping boxes small with minimal overlap.
Although rebuilding costs (O(nlogn)), it is inexpensive with
only 100 circles

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
Circles near window edges get clipped because their centers
can be at the screen boundaries while their radii extend beyond
them. This is not caused by the BVH.
